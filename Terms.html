CreatorFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

CreatorFlow ("we", "our", or "the Service") is a content management platform designed to help users manage and organize their social media content and TikTok-related activities.

1. Information We Collect

When you connect your TikTok account to CreatorFlow, we may receive information that you authorize TikTok to share with us, including:

  • Basic TikTok profile information
  • TikTok display name and profile information
  • Profile statistics, where authorized
  • Public video information, where authorized
  • TikTok account authorization information

We may also collect information that you voluntarily provide while using CreatorFlow, such as content information, scheduling information, and settings related to your use of the Service.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information only for purposes related to providing and improving CreatorFlow, including:

  • Connecting your TikTok account to CreatorFlow
  • Displaying your authorized TikTok profile information
  • Managing and organizing your content
  • Supporting content upload and publishing features
  • Providing scheduling and content management functionality
  • Improving the reliability and functionality of the Service
  • Responding to support requests

3. TikTok Data

CreatorFlow uses TikTok's authorized APIs and only accesses TikTok information that the user has permitted through the TikTok authorization process.

We do not sell TikTok user information or use TikTok information for purposes unrelated to providing the requested functionality of CreatorFlow.

4. Account Authorization

When you connect your TikTok account, you authorize CreatorFlow to access the permissions that you approve through TikTok's authorization screen.

You may revoke access to CreatorFlow through the applicable TikTok account settings or authorization controls.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by CreatorFlow against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Authentication credentials, access tokens, and other sensitive authorization information should not be publicly disclosed.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain the Service, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve disputes.

Where information is no longer required for these purposes, it may be deleted or securely disposed of.

7. Third-Party Services

CreatorFlow may use third-party infrastructure and services to operate the platform, such as hosting, databases, authentication, automation, and API services.

Such services may process information only as necessary to provide their respective functions and operate the Service.

8. Children's Privacy

CreatorFlow is not intended for children who are not permitted to use the Service under applicable laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable requirements.
